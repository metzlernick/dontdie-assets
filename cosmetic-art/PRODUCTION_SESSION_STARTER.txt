IMPORTANT PRODUCTION ARCHITECTURE

Do NOT try to achieve pixel-perfect hand/finger registration during image generation.

AI Stage A owns:
- design
- style
- approximate body-relative scale
- approximate position
- broad orientation
- registration-safe object geometry

AI Stage B owns:
- cosmetic isolation
- removal of hero/hand/body/pedestal
- preservation/reconstruction of continuous usable object geometry

Illustrator Stage C owns:
- exact X/Y
- exact final scale/rotation
- exact canonical hand/finger overlay
- exact grip registration
- final cleanup

For held objects, generated hand geometry is contextual/disposable.

The cosmetic must contain enough continuous grip/attachment geometry to tolerate a modest Illustrator move/rotation beneath the canonical hand.

Do NOT create:
- hand-shaped holes
- fake transparent grip cutouts
- overly long handles merely for tolerance
- oversized objects merely to fill the cell

Hanging objects:
- provide usable attachment geometry near the hand
- then allow a bend/transition
- main body obeys gravity and hangs vertically

Use the full MAIN HERO registration sheet for Stage A when it materially improves scale and body-relative placement.

Evaluate Stage A based on whether the OBJECT can be finalized with modest Illustrator registration—not whether generated fingers are exact.

After source review, report the normal compliance summary and proceed with the supplied category/rows.
