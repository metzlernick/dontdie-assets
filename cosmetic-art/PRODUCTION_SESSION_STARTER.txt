LEFT ARM v7.54 architecture:

- Show canonical MAIN HERO on screen-right as scale reference.
- Generate the complete cosmetic separately on screen-left.
- Maintain clear white zero-contact separation between item and hero.
- Never place the item under/over/in the hero hand during generation.
- Never hide object geometry behind hero pixels.
- Do not use image-generation Stage B reconstruction/isolation.
- Do not require Illustrator reconstruction of missing geometry.
- Object must have a crisp continuous black outline and be Image-Trace friendly.
- Spatula, money bag, and lantern v7.54 scales are validated.
