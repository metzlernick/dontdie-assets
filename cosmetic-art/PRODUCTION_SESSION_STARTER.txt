LEFT ARM PRODUCTION ARCHITECTURE IS LOCKED:
- one generation pass for up to four current briefs
- 2400×2560 sheet; 4×4; 600×640 logical cells
- tiny finished cosmetic exemplar is the visual scale/style authority
- hero context screen-right; complete cosmetic screen-left
- clean white zero-contact separation
- no generated grip, hidden geometry, or AI reconstruction pass
- replacement should occupy approximately the SAME OR SMALLER total visual mass as the exemplar
- the exemplar is NOT merely a position marker
- permanent bias: WHEN UNCERTAIN, SMALLER
- slightly undersized source art is preferred because Illustrator enlargement is easy
- production priority: FINISHED-EXEMPLAR SCALE/LOCATION → SIMPLIFY DETAIL TO FIT SCALE → EXACT IDENTITY → POSE/DESIGN/VARIATION
- IF SIZE AND DETAIL CONFLICT: KEEP SMALL SIZE AND REMOVE/SIMPLIFY DETAIL
- real-world size and comparison-asset size do not override production scale
- approved references may control palette/design language/silhouette vocabulary, but not generation scale

MANDATORY CONFLICT GATE — LEFT ARM
Before creating any LEFT ARM production package, compare every user instruction against the locked controller.

If the user asks for or implies a change to scale, exemplar authority, hero placement, zero-contact, sheet geometry, detail hierarchy, grip/hand interaction, or reconstruction workflow:
1. STOP before producing files.
2. Explain the exact conflict and likely failure mode.
3. Offer a safe rewording that preserves the design intent without modifying the canonical production system.
4. Proceed only after the brief is non-conflicting.

Do not silently reinterpret a conflict.
Do not invent a one-off exception.
Do not redesign the controller for a specific row.
Do not let phrases such as "large," "same scale as," or "similar size to" alter canonical LEFT ARM generation scale.
