# DON'T DIE COSMETIC ART — v6.6 MAIN HERO REGISTRATION

This supersedes the dot-anchor HAT calibration method.

Use `reference-sheets/HAT_MAIN_HERO_REGISTRATION_4X4.png`.

The exact MAIN HERO is present at approximately 7% opacity in every canonical 480×640 logical cell of the 1920×2560 sheet.

Generate every HAT directly ON the faint hero at its FINAL production position, FINAL production scale, and correct orientation.

Do not center the cosmetic in empty space. Do not normalize cosmetic sizes. Empty white space is irrelevant.

The faint MAIN HERO supplies the exact head size, hairline, ears, pose, and slight rightward-facing bias. Do not redraw, redesign, reinterpret, move, resize, rotate, or restyle the registration character.

Directional headwear must inherit the hero geometry. In particular, headphones must wrap around the actual head/ear locations and must not be mirrored into a generic straight-on view.

STYLE:
- approved Don't Die style
- true-black bold outer stroke visually matching the approved 5 px SVG stroke at canonical scale
- consistent stroke width regardless of cosmetic size
- flat hard-edged solid fills
- minimal interior black lines
- low detail
- no gradients
- no soft shading
- no feathering
- no glossy/painterly rendering
- no ambient/cast/drop shadows
- no texture/noise

For this calibration test ONLY, leave the faint MAIN HERO visible. Judge fit, scale, orientation, stroke, and style. Cosmetic-only extraction happens after the overlay fit is approved.
