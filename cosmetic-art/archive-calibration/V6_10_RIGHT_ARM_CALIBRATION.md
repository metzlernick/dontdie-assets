# DON'T DIE COSMETIC ART — V6.10 RIGHT ARM CALIBRATION

## Purpose
Calibrate the successful two-stage registration → isolation workflow for RIGHT ARM cosmetics.

## Canonical geometry
- Sheet: 1920×2560 px
- 4×4 logical layout
- Cell: 480×640 px
- No gutters
- MAIN HERO defines position, pose, scale, and orientation.
- Exact canonical RIGHT-HAND grip position is sacred.
- Never presentation-center or enlarge an item merely to fill white space.

## Stage A — visible registration
Generate the FULL MAIN HERO in every populated cell as the spatial registration mannequin.
Generate the RIGHT ARM object directly through/behind the hero's canonical right hand at its final production scale, angle, and position.

The hand may cover the object's grip during Stage A. This is desirable because it proves the grip geometry works.

Do not move the hero or invent a new pose to accommodate the object.
Do not rotate/mirror the hero.
Do not move the hand toward the weapon.
The WEAPON must conform to the canonical hand position.

The hero is disposable registration artwork. Exact hero pixel preservation is not the acceptance criterion; object geometry relative to the canonical hero is.

## Stage B — isolation
After Stage A is approved, remove MAIN HERO and all character artwork.
Retain ONLY the RIGHT ARM object in each cell, at the same registered production position and scale.

IMPORTANT grip rule:
- remove the hand
- do NOT leave an empty hand-shaped/grip-shaped hole
- reconstruct/preserve the object continuously through the grip region
- the final production hand will later be layered over this region

## Style lock
- Don't Die approved-art visual language
- flat 2D vector-style forms
- hard-edged solid color regions
- 5 px true-black primary outer stroke at canonical 480×640 scale
- absolute stroke width across objects, not proportional scaling
- round caps/joins
- minimal interior linework; approximately 3–4 px where genuinely needed
- low detail
- no cast shadows
- no texture/noise
- no soft painterly rendering
- gradients forbidden unless the current row explicitly overrides the rule

Amorphous effects such as flame do not require a full thick black perimeter. Physical flamethrower body still uses the normal 5 px black outline.

## Calibration rows

### ROW 1 — Pirate Cutlass
A silver, pirate-style standard cutlass with a piece of tattered red cloth ribbon hanging from the handle.

Variation dimensions may include:
- modest blade curvature/proportion
- guard silhouette
- handle treatment
- ribbon shape/tattering

Keep it recognizably a conventional pirate cutlass. Silver blade, restrained grey hard-edged shading, red tattered cloth.

### ROW 2 — Frying Pan
A standard black frying pan with a handle deliberately long enough that it remains visibly identifiable as a handle even while MAIN HERO's right hand overlays the grip region.

Use restrained dark-grey hard-edged shapes so the pan does not read as a featureless black circle.

Variation dimensions may include:
- pan bowl proportions
- rim treatment
- subtle hard-edged grey highlight placement
- handle-end shape

It must remain an ordinary frying pan, not a fantasy weapon redesign.

### ROW 3 — Flamethrower
An epic grey/black steel flamethrower with red reflective/accent regions and a burst of flames shooting from the top/nozzle.

Physical weapon:
- 5 px true-black outer stroke
- flat hard-edged steel/black/red forms
- no painterly metal rendering

Flame effect:
- explicitly exempt from the full thick-black perimeter requirement
- may use clean layered yellow/orange/red flame shapes
- keep edges crisp rather than smoky/feathered

The weapon must visibly connect to the canonical right-hand grip and read as a usable handheld flamethrower.

### ROW 4 — Energy Sword
A Halo-inspired energy-sword concept with purple, pink, and blue hues.

EXPLICIT STYLE OVERRIDE FOR THIS ROW ONLY:
- gradient color and luminous glow are allowed for the ENERGY BLADE
- this overrides the normal no-gradient/no-glow rule
- the physical hilt remains consistent with Don't Die flat vector styling and normal black outline
- glow should remain controlled and localized; do not wash out the white sheet or obscure the grip

Interpret the concept rather than copying a specific copyrighted sword design exactly. Preserve the recognizable idea of a futuristic split/pronged luminous energy blade.

## Stage A acceptance criteria
1. Exactly 16 populated designs: four variations per row.
2. Full MAIN HERO visible in every cell.
3. Same canonical hero position/scale/pose across all cells as closely as generation permits.
4. Every object intersects the exact canonical right-hand grip.
5. Correct right-arm orientation; no mirroring.
6. Realistic relative scale for the hero, not icon scale.
7. Hand visibly overlays the grip where appropriate.
8. 5 px black object outline matches approved production art.
9. Row 3 flame outline exception honored.
10. Row 4 blade gradient/glow exception honored only there.
11. No visible grid, labels, guides, anchors, or registration dots.

## Stage B acceptance criteria
1. Character completely removed.
2. Exactly 16 RIGHT ARM objects remain.
3. Object X/Y, scale, angle, and silhouette remain production-equivalent to approved Stage A.
4. Grip regions contain continuous object artwork after hand removal.
5. Pure white background.
6. No guides or extraction marks.
