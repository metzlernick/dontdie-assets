FINISHED-EXEMPLAR SCALE AUTHORITY

Use:
reference-sheets/LEFT_ARM_FINISHED_COSMETIC_EXEMPLAR_REFERENCE_4X4.png

Each cell contains a tiny neutral outlined finished-cosmetic exemplar on screen-left.
Replace the exemplar with the assigned cosmetic. Do not reproduce the exemplar itself.

The exemplar controls intended final cosmetic visual mass, small-side bias, outline behavior, simplicity, and relationship to MAIN HERO.

The replacement should occupy approximately the SAME OR SMALLER total visual mass as the exemplar.
When uncertain, choose smaller.

Do not enlarge because:
- the real-world object is large
- an approved reference asset is larger
- another cosmetic is a similar shape
- empty space remains
- optional detail needs room
- the brief uses words such as large, big, full-size, same size as, or similar scale to

Existing approved art may guide palette, design language, silhouette vocabulary, material treatment, thematic family, and non-scale proportion cues. It may not override canonical LEFT ARM generation scale.

DETAIL MUST FOLLOW SCALE

Establish production size first, then simplify until the object reads at that size.
If optional detail requires enlargement, remove or simplify the detail.

Use few major shapes, sparse interior lines, few color regions, large readable identity cues, no micro-decoration, and no texture-like detail.

SCALE → SIMPLIFY DETAIL → EXACT IDENTITY → POSE/DESIGN/VARIATION.

OBJECT-SHAPE BEHAVIOR

- compact/simple: at or below exemplar visual mass
- long/thin/hanging: extra extent permitted only by staying narrow and low-mass
- creatures/character-like: strong miniature bias and simplified anatomy/face
- bottles/containers/multi-part handheld: strong miniature bias and minimal secondary construction
- broad objects: may widen naturally but remain tiny relative to MAIN HERO

ROW IDENTITY

Every cell in a populated row must remain the exact requested object type.
Required features repeat in every applicable variation unless explicitly stated otherwise.
Do not transfer features between rows.
Do not substitute generic fantasy defaults.

VARIATION

Four useful but simple interpretations per populated row.
Variation may change modest silhouette/proportions and a few major design details.
Variation may not increase scale or complexity enough to violate the finished-exemplar visual-mass rule.

ZERO-CONTACT — MANDATORY

Every cosmetic must:
- stay viewer-left / screen-left
- remain separated from every hero pixel by clean white space
- not touch or overlap hero, hand, cape, body, pedestal, or shadow
- remain complete and unobstructed
- contain standalone geometry for later Illustrator placement

The hero must not hold or interact with the cosmetic during generation.

STYLE

Match approved Don't Die production art:
- flat vector-like cartoon
- bold smooth true-black outer contour, visually about 5 px at canonical scale
- rounded joins/caps
- sparse interior linework
- hard-edged solid colors
- extremely low detail appropriate for tiny display
- no cosmetic cast shadow
- no texture/noise
- no painterly rendering
- no gradients unless explicitly requested and not scale-inducing
- Illustrator/Image-Trace friendly

FINAL CHECK

Before generation verify:
- all row briefs are identity/design descriptions only
- no row brief has been converted into a scale/placement/controller exception
- approved references affect design, not generation scale
- all rows remain governed by the same canonical exemplar authority
- all cosmetics are screen-left, complete, zero-contact, tiny, and simple
