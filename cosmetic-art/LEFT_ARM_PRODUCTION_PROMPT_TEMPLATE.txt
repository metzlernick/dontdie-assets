FINISHED-EXEMPLAR SCALE AUTHORITY — V3

Use `reference-sheets/LEFT_ARM_FINISHED_COSMETIC_EXEMPLAR_REFERENCE_4X4.png`.

Each cell contains ONE extremely tiny contiguous neutral outlined finished-cosmetic exemplar on screen-left, approximately 14×19 px in the validated reference. Replace it with the assigned cosmetic. Do not reproduce exemplar geometry.

The exemplar is the ABSOLUTE FIRST-PRIORITY visual authority for final cosmetic visual mass. It is not merely a location marker. The replacement must remain in the same extremely tiny finished-art scale neighborhood relative to MAIN HERO and should match or undercut the exemplar's visual mass.

Semantic identity never authorizes enlargement. Do not scale a gauntlet, weapon, throwing star, purse, nunchuk, shield, bag, spatula, lantern, wearable, handheld object, creature, container, or any other cosmetic according to its normal real-world or readable-prop size.

Do not enlarge because an approved reference is larger, empty space remains, details need room, or the brief uses words such as large/big/full-size/same size/similar scale. Existing approved art may guide palette, design language, silhouette vocabulary, material treatment, thematic family, and non-scale proportion cues only.

Do not reproduce detached reference-like artifacts. Unless explicitly identity-critical, do not add dots, beads, particles, connector marks, trails, or floating secondary shapes between cosmetic and hero.
