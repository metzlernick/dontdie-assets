# Don't Die Cosmetic Art — Production Package Regression Gates

**Status: mandatory pre-generation tests.**

A package is not ready merely because it contains the right files. It must preserve the validated category generation setup. These gates do not redesign category architectures.

## Universal packaging conditions

FAIL if generic packaging adds active visual references solely because they exist in the permanent kit, changes a category's validated controller/version, substitutes a generic reference sheet, or silently rewrites a locked prompt outside permitted brief substitutions.

Approved-art/design references are subordinate to the category's spatial/registration authority and control only the design properties explicitly assigned to them.

When investigating a regression: compare against that category's last validated setup, identify the concrete packaging/execution difference, make the smallest supported correction, and do not propagate speculative fixes to other categories.

## LEFT ARM V3 — locked architecture

LEFT ARM V3 retains the validated **one-pass zero-contact generation → Illustrator extraction** architecture. There is no AI Stage B/isolation pass.

The generated sheet is source art for downstream Illustrator extraction. Therefore these are **not, by themselves, LEFT ARM failures**:

- MAIN HERO remains visibly rendered in the generated sheet;
- hero/controller pixels are not literally preserved by generation;
- generated raster dimensions differ from the canonical controller dimensions;
- minor raster reconstruction of hero/context that does not interfere with cosmetic source art.

Do **not** require deterministic composition, literal controller-pixel preservation, hero removal, or a new AI isolation stage as LEFT ARM acceptance criteria.

### LEFT ARM acceptance criteria

- 4×4 organization: one requested cosmetic family per row and four useful variations per row.
- MAIN HERO context remains screen-right; cosmetic source art remains screen-left.
- Clear white **zero-contact** separation: no grip, overlap, hidden geometry, or contact-dependent reconstruction.
- `LEFT_ARM_FINISHED_COSMETIC_EXEMPLAR_REFERENCE_4X4_V3.png` remains the V3 scale/location authority. Target the validated tiny source-art neighborhood rather than normal prop readability.
- Requested identity, orientation, pose, color, and row-specific details must survive in all four variations unless the brief explicitly permits variation.
- DESIGN_ONLY references cannot override V3 scale/location/zero-contact behavior.
- Real-world adjectives such as large, broad, substantial, or long describe silhouette/proportions unless the brief explicitly overrides production scale.

### Regression A — shield / money bag / spatula / lantern

1. Golden ceremonial shield matching Gold Armor + Gold Sword design language; broad Mirror Shield-inspired silhouette while remaining in the validated V3 tiny source-art range.
2. Tan money bag with dollar sign; **the top/neck of the bag must visibly tilt slightly left in all four variations**.
3. Standard metal spatula; silver metal head at top-left, black handle toward bottom-right. **Do not infer or enforce slots unless the active brief explicitly requests a slotted spatula.**
4. Small spooky black lantern hanging downward with a green flame inside; restrained internal green gradient is permitted when requested.

Evaluation notes for Regression A:

- The previously observed overall accessory scale is acceptable.
- Shield and lantern may be **modestly larger** when that improves identity/readability while preserving zero-contact and the validated tiny LEFT ARM range.
- Visible hero is expected and is not a failure.
- Raster output dimensions alone are not a failure.
- Money-bag neck direction is a hard local constraint and must visibly tilt slightly left in all four columns.

### Regression B — difficult V3 control

The established gauntlet / throwing star / purse / nunchuks batch remains the difficult V3 control. Evaluate it with the same LEFT ARM criteria above. Do not add hero-removal, literal-pixel-preservation, deterministic-composition, or raster-dimension gates.

## Other categories

HAT, RIGHT ARM, ARMOR/OUTFITS, and ACCESSORY retain their own validated workflows and category-specific acceptance gates. LEFT ARM's Illustrator extraction architecture must not be generalized to them, and their post-processing/isolation behavior must not be imposed on LEFT ARM.

A LEFT ARM result is not evidence for changing another category. Audit another category only against its own validated control when there is evidence of a packaging/execution regression in that category.
