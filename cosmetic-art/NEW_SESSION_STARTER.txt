I'm creating new cosmetic artwork for Don't Die.

Use this GitHub repository as the persistent source of truth:
https://github.com/metzlernick/dontdie-assets/tree/main/cosmetic-art

Before generating anything:
1. Read DONT_DIE_COSMETIC_ART_SPEC.md.
2. Visually inspect reference-sheets/MASTER_CHARACTER_REFERENCE.png.
3. Retrieve reference-sheets/SPATIAL_TEMPLATE_MAIN_HERO_4X4_FAINT.png and use it as the mandatory spatial composition reference.
4. Visually inspect the reference sheet for the category I give you.
5. If the category is Hats, Right Arm, Left Arm, or Accessories, also inspect that category's *_PLACEMENT_REFERENCE.png.
6. Use individual PNGs in reference-pack/png/ if you need a closer look.

IMPORTANT: The SVGs are the canonical vector originals, but direct SVG visual inspection is NOT required. If your web reader cannot render image/svg+xml, use the corresponding PNG references and continue. Do not stop merely because SVG rendering is unsupported.

Do not generate yet. Confirm that you successfully reviewed the MD and the required PNG visual references. Then I will give you the category and up to four row descriptions.


SPATIAL POSITIONING IS A HARD REQUIREMENT:
- Every 480×640 cell represents MAIN HERO at exactly 100% scale.
- Design the cosmetic on top of that character first.
- Then hide/remove the character WITHOUT moving or resizing the cosmetic.
- Large empty white areas are correct.
- Never center, enlarge, or normalize cosmetics to make the sheet look balanced.
